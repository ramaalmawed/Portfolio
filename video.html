Video Editing

A collection of video editing and motion graphics projects created using Adobe Premiere Pro and Adobe After Effects. These projects focus on storytelling, pacing, sound, motion design, and visual communication.

Spikön — Documentary Edit

A documentary-style video about Spikön in Trollhättan, focusing on local storytelling, atmosphere, pacing, footage selection, and sound.

Local Documentary Storytelling

In this project, I edited a documentary-style video about Spikön. I worked with pacing, scene order, transitions, sound, and visual rhythm to create a calm and engaging local story.

The focus was not only on showing the place, but also on creating a feeling through editing choices, audio, and visual flow.

Tools: Adobe Premiere Pro · Video Editing · Sound Design

Infographic — Motion Design

An animated infographic created in Adobe After Effects, focusing on motion graphics, timing, animated visuals, and clear information design.

Animated Information Design

This project translates information into motion. I worked with animated elements, movement, timing, and transitions to make the content easier to understand and more visually engaging.

The goal was to combine graphic design and animation in a way that supports the message instead of distracting from it.

Tools: Adobe After Effects · Motion Graphics · Animation

Group Project — Motion Graphics

A collaborative video project created with a group using Adobe After Effects, focusing on animation, teamwork, creative direction, and motion design.

Collaborative Motion Design

This group project gave me experience working in a creative production team. I contributed to the visual style, motion design, and animation process while adapting my work to a shared concept.

The project helped me practice collaboration, consistency, timing, and how to make separate animated parts feel like one cohesive video.

Tools: Adobe After Effects · Collaboration · Motion Design

Video & Audio Production

During my Digital Media studies at University West, I also worked on a collaborative university production project that gave me experience with the wider video production process — from planning and recording to editing and presenting in front of the camera.

Working Behind & In Front of the Camera

As part of the project, I worked together with a group to plan, film, record, and produce video content. This gave me practical experience with different parts of a production rather than focusing only on post-production.

I gained experience working behind the camera with filming and recording, while also experiencing what it is like to be in front of the camera. Working from both perspectives helped me better understand how planning, performance, camera work, sound, and editing come together in the final production.

Editing, Audio & Storytelling

For post-production, I worked with Adobe Premiere Pro for video editing and Adobe Audition for audio editing. This included organizing footage, selecting suitable clips, working with pacing and transitions, and improving the audio to support the final video.

Storytelling was also an important part of the process. We had to think about what we wanted to communicate, how scenes should be structured, and how image, sound, dialogue, and editing could work together to create a clear and engaging story.
